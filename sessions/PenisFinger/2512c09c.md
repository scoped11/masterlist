## What's different vs rtcore

Corsair's `MmMapIoSpace` full-page mapping into user VA means one IOCTL per 4 KiB page + one memcpy. TRIXX and RTCore64 do one IOCTL per 8 bytes. On the EPROCESS scan (needs to read every 4 KiB page), Corsair should be **~500× faster** on wall-clock. Also Corsair is on WHQL (not just author-signed like RTCore64's MSI cert), so trust chain is different — but per the earlier research it's still on Microsoft's VDBL, so the same "offline VM only, disable VDBL" applies.
